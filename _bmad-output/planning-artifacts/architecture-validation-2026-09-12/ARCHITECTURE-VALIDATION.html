Critical findings

CriticalVAL3-C01 — Persisted roll-up contract unbound

  • Issue: AD-06/22 never bind the persisted slot DTO, keys, atomic visibility, equal-sequence rule, writer ownership, merge seam, or materialization; live state uses one watermark and whole-document replacement.
  • Divergence/risk: Works and EventStore can implement incompatible layouts that each satisfy the formula; replacement can silently erase another slot or own fields.
  • Source: architecture.md:82–95, :263, :318–342.
  • Corroboration: WorkItemRollUp.cs:29; WorkItemProjectionDispatcher.cs:440; work-roll-up-projection.md:94.
  • Found by: ADV-0912-01; CONF-0912-06.
  • Disposition: Discuss, then autofix in Update. Choose and bind exact DTO/key/merge/materialization ownership; assign a replacing story before AD-22.

CriticalVAL3-C02 — “Event alone” cannot produce contribution

  • Issue: ProgressReported is delta-only, ReEstimated lacks prior Done, and terminal contribution needs folded lifecycle state; Raw-Act rules prohibit derived totals in those payloads.
  • Divergence/risk: Raw-event, folded-state, and enriched-payload implementations write different ancestor totals after normal histories.
  • Source: :82–103, :320–337, :782–800.
  • Corroboration: ProgressReported.cs:8; ReEstimated.cs:8; WorkItemRollUpProjection.cs:222.
  • Found by: ADV-0912-02; RW12-03; CONF-0912-06.
  • Disposition: Autofix after contract confirmation. A Works folded projection emits an absolute snapshot; R4 transports it without interpreting payloads.

CriticalVAL3-C03 — No single child-creation and attachment evidence chain

  • Issue: AD-21 attaches on ChildSpawned, repair asks for child-stream evidence, the PRD requires WorkItemCreated, and no current event carries reservation/witness identity.
  • Divergence/risk: Units can produce an attached edge without a child, or a child the registry does not recognize; replay/timeout follows different authorities.
  • Source: :272–316, :327–333, prd.md:287–296.
  • Corroboration: WorkItemAggregate.cs:71; WorkItemCreated.cs:8; ChildSpawned.cs:8.
  • Found by: ADV-0912-03; RW12-02/05; CONF-0912-03/08.
  • Disposition: Discuss, then autofix spine and PRD addendum. Recommended: fenced Reactor create; matching WorkItemCreated is sole attach evidence; registry envelope sequence is the witness.

CriticalVAL3-C04 — Reserved watermark can acknowledge empty ancestry

  • Issue: AD-22 says watermark ≥ reservation sequence is fresh, but AD-21 enumerates only Attached edges; immediately after reservation, the predicate is true while ancestry is empty.
  • Divergence/risk: The only child delivery can be acknowledged before attach and never repaired; arrival order changes final roll-up state.
  • Source: :293–302, :327–335.
  • Corroboration: Registry is unbuilt; current projection is single-aggregate at WorkItemProjectionDispatcher.cs:96.
  • Found by: ADV-0912-04; corroborated by RW12-03.
  • Disposition: Autofix before story drafting. Require the exact matching Attached transition/sequence, or make attach deterministically backfill contribution.

CriticalVAL3-C05 — Recovery skips every page-boundary event

  • Issue: EventStore's cursor is exclusive; two recovery readers add one to the returned last sequence, skipping the next event.
  • Divergence/risk: Boundary child, await, resume, or terminal facts silently disappear; checkpoints become permanently incomplete without health/retry evidence.
  • Source: :119–135 (AD-09/10), :266 (R7). It is an architecture finding specifically because it violates these adopted rules.
  • Corroboration: StreamReadRequest.cs:8; Cascade reader:75; Child-completion reader:127.
  • Found by: SEC-DI-0912-01; CONF-0912-01.
  • Disposition: Autofix code/tests immediately, then revalidate. Reuse the returned last sequence and add >200-event boundary/restart tests. No fix is applied here.

  • Issue: Exact registry identity, topic, route, reserved ID, registration, translators, timeout, policy enrichment, and sole topology interface are unbound.

  • Issue: VAL-H10 remains open; originators derive IDs differently, Works leaves semantic idempotency null, and default retention is 24 hours.
  • Risk: Saga effects can duplicate after retry, migration, retention expiry, backup, or DR replay.
