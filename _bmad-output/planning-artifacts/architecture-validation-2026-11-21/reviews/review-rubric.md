# Architecture checklist review

Reviewed the November 21 spine at SHA-256 `e53447b83e031c5ef39e9f1e443cdf311a7db164d31f43a36bb0b3693a7609ab`, against Works `07020c84` and its November 20 PRD. **Verdict: amend the historical completion rule and two lifecycle contract details.** The findings affect how independently built writers and readers interpret the same events. Each has a resolution in the existing product contract.

## Findings

| Severity | Decision | Evidence and impact | Proposed disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | AD-07, spine lines 179–187 | AD-05 correctly allows a positive report to complete an item whose Remaining was already zero. AD-07 infers a historical completion kind from a preceding “progress-to-zero transition.” A reader looking for a change from positive to zero would miss an overrun report. `WorkItemEffort.Report` preserves the overrun and `WorkItemAggregate.Handle(ReportProgress)` emits `ProgressReported` and `WorkItemCompleted` together whenever the resulting Remaining is zero (aggregate lines 321–327). The current `WorkItemCompleted` record has no kind field. A mistaken explicit classification would bar the correction that FR-8 permits. | Bind historical progress completion to the accepted report's paired completion, including zero-to-zero. Rehearse a re-estimate below Done, a positive report, replay, and a correction restoring positive Remaining. Include explicit completion at zero as a distinct control. |
| Medium | AD-04, spine lines 129–135, and AD-21, lines 419–427 | FR-12's normative truth table fixes an explicit or inherited Unit **at spawn** even when the child has no estimate. A child whose parent and payload both lack a Unit remains unitless. The spine says the first estimate fixes Unit. A Registry producer can omit the spawn-time fact and a later consumer can consult a parent that has since acquired a Unit, changing which first estimate the child accepts. | Preserve the explicit, inherited, or absent Unit established at spawn in the reserved child facts and creation evidence. Enforce that fact on the first estimate. Include the parent that was unitless at spawn in conformance vectors. See PRD lines 279–290. |
| Medium | AD-14, spine lines 262–270 | “An item has one ExecutorBinding” makes binding mandatory for states where FR-17 permits an unbound item. `CreateWorkItem` line 26 defaults `ExecutorBinding?` to null. `WorkItemState.Apply(WorkItemCreated)` lines 113–119 preserves it. A reader enforcing exactly one binding on every item could reject valid Created history or synthesize an executor. | State that an item has at most one binding. Assigned, InProgress, and Suspended require a responsible Party. Verify unbound creation/replay plus assignment and claim using the same schema. See PRD line 367. |

## Other checklist results

- **Coverage and ownership.** The capability map covers FR-1 through FR-26. AD-03 separates queue eligibility from approved active-item visibility. AD-10/21 distinguish current process execution from registry arbitration, late waits and reopening. The document identifies the per-tenant migration and two-parent restart proof. The domain, SDK and Platform own distinct parts of the deployment. No parent spine is declared.
- **Enforceability and deferrals.** The remaining decisions identify a concrete writer, admission rule, durable outcome or qualification owner. Security, source pairing, registry concurrency, data lifecycle and G8 performance qualification have explicit prerequisites. Deferred UI, notification delivery, provider choice and data-retention duration each have an owner or revisit condition. These open gates describe delivery work and do not themselves reduce the architecture rating.
- **Brownfield and versions.** Cross-checked `.NET 11.0.100` with `global.json`, EventStore `5.4.0` and PolymorphicSerializations `2.0.0` with the catalog at Builds `fe68718b`, and the source pairs against `git ls-tree 07020c84` and `tools/source-debug-dependencies.json`. The spine reports the recorded-source drift. Reminder actor registration, superseded callback disposition and exclusive stream paging agree with `WorksRecoveryExtensions.AddWorksReminderAndCascadeRecovery`, the SDK `ReminderCoordinator.RetireStaleAsync`, and `WorksSourceStreamReader.ReadCoreAsync`.
- **Operations.** AD-20/24/27/28 and the readiness table cover environment ownership, production admission, operator authority, restore, degraded readiness and recovery. Historical cutover evidence is scoped to its producer. This review inspected sources and retained evidence. It did not run a fresh fleet qualification.
