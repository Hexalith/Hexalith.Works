# Works technical audit and sprint change proposal

20 November 2026 · Review of `main` at `80314acf` · Approved scope includes BMAD artifacts and product fixes.

## Executive summary

Works is a headless work-item domain for tenant members, executors and Platform integrators. It records obligations, assignments, progress, scheduling and child-work events. Its pure decision and replay code, separate query projections and five-package inventory make important behavior independently testable. Extensive SDK integration coverage complements those tests. The highest-impact changes are to preserve recorded effort across planning changes, bind every incoming command to its stream, and regain exact-commit Platform qualification before release.

The domain host starts in `src/Hexalith.Works/Program.cs` and registers EventStore and the Works adapters in `src/Hexalith.Works/Runtime/WorksHost.cs:19`. SDK dispatch selects one of the typed work-item handlers. The pure aggregate returns events, EventStore appends them, and WorkItemState folds them on replay. The projection handler builds the tenant queue, per-item roll-up and recovery documents. What's Next reads the selected generation. The reminder and cascade workers consult source events before submitting effects back through EventStore. External dependencies include EventStore, Dapr, Parties identity lookup, Platform hosting and the Builds SDK/catalog. See `src/Hexalith.Works.Server/Aggregates/WorkItemAggregate.cs`, `src/Hexalith.Works.Contracts/State/WorkItemState.cs`, `src/Hexalith.Works/Queries/WhatsNextQueryHandler.cs` and `src/Hexalith.Works/Recovery/WorksSourceStreamReader.cs`.

The query path adds tenant and Parties authority checks to the SDK policy. Failure diagnostics record a bounded category, tenant identifier and correlation information. NuGet restore audits transitive packages across all severities, with advisory warnings visible and non-blocking. The local package scan reported no known advisories across the 11 evaluated projects at review time. Production identity policy, transport settings, log retention and a representative load profile were outside the evidence available here. See `src/Hexalith.Works/Runtime/WorksDomainAdapters.cs:52`, `src/Hexalith.Works/Runtime/WorksFailureDiagnostics.cs` and `Directory.Build.props:61`.

## Rollout and recovery

Deploy the write-side and read-side effort interpretations together. With writes quiesced in a disposable restored copy, replay raw events for streams containing a progress event followed by a lower estimate. Compare aggregate, roll-up and What's Next values before and after raising the estimate again. Check Status, Unit and stream positions. An old snapshot containing only the clamped Done cannot recover the lost value on its own.

Before production rollout, the EventStore operator must identify whether any affected aggregate snapshots are served and demonstrate a supported full-event rehydration for them on the restored copy. Rebuild the disposable projection family into a fresh epoch, drain catch-up and check active-generation queries before reopening traffic. Invalidate any external response caches for affected items. If the deployment cannot replay from raw events, hold this rollout. Preserve event streams and a matched pre-change fleet/store backup for rollback. No production inventory or migration operation was available in this review.

Platform operators can collect the `Hexalith.Works.Projections` meter. Use `works.catchup.history.size` to compare samples with the 16 MiB tenant boundary and `works.catchup.changed_documents` with the 128 changed-document boundary. An initial staging alert threshold of 80% is a proposed starting point. Evaluate sustained high percentiles alongside capacity failures and `works.catchup.success.duration`. The latter covers successful folds through operation selection, excluding the subsequent SDK commit. Instruments carry no tenant, item or payload labels.

Rolling back Platform would restore an older producer combination and lose its fixes. Reducing the MVP would leave the data-integrity defect exposed. Preserve current sprint tracking, including 4.9, until the independent readiness review passes. The product owner authorized implementation in this turn.
