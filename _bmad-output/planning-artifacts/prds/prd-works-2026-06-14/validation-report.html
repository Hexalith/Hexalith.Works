This is a strategically coherent, unusually substantive technical PRD with explicit scope cuts, product bets, testable consequences, and useful counter-metrics. It is not yet safe as an unqualified implementation contract: normative sections disagree on core lifecycle, tree, authorization, and Roll-Up behavior, and the addendum contradicts the security-critical actor-provenance rule.

The adversarial, downstream-drift, and readiness passes confirm that coordinated implementation is blocked. The PRD was amended after the prior validation, but architecture and epics still encode the earlier 25-FR design. Isolated pure-domain work may proceed only where the PRD is internally consistent. Across four reviews, 52 finding occurrences consolidate to 5 critical, 13 high, 6 medium, and 3 low issues.

Decision-readiness

adequate

The PRD exposes real decisions: the kernel-only cut, carried-but-unenforced authority, eventual cross-aggregate effects, and no active-work reassignment. It remains unsafe as a clean build/release authorization while normative rules conflict and required platform gates are scattered.

Normative Resume behavior has two answers

§4.2 FR-6; §4.4 FR-15
The table rejects Resume from InProgress, while FR-15 requires a consumed-condition replay to be a no-op after the item returns to InProgress.
Fix: Make FR-15 the single rule and mirror its three outcomes in the table, architecture, epics, and tests.

§9; §13; addendum handoff
