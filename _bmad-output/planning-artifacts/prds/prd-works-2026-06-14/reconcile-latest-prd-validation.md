# Reconciliation of the 20 November 2026 PRD validation

The validation recorded six high, five medium and two low findings against the Works PRD. This update records both Product decisions made during reconciliation and identifies the SDK behavior that still needs to change. The validation report preserves the findings as originally raised. ## Cross-aggregate effects and lifecycle

| Finding | What the sources establish | Consequence for the PRD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1. Effect-key conflict | AD-26 binds a receipt to its complete effect tuple, semantic command digest and recorded disposition. Replay may reproduce success, rejection or no-op. | Require matching key, tuple and semantics for receipt replay. A collision quarantines the conflict without changing the target or treating an earlier result as success. Keep this target qualification under G1. |
| H2. Parent bookkeeping | AD-21 defines admission before reservation and a second state check at attachment. A request with a wait requires an InProgress or Suspended parent at admission. An identical retry is idempotent. | With the wait flag clear, record the attachment and preserve the parent’s status and waits. With it set, suspend an InProgress parent or add a missing child condition to a Suspended parent without reordering or normalizing its existing collection. A parent that became terminal stays terminal. Cancelled and Expired require late-child cascade catch-up. |
| H6. Child completes early | FR-26 requires recovery of missed child-completion resumes. AD-09, AD-10, AD-21 and AD-27 allow reordering across streams and require durable recovery. The existing child-completion translator reads the committed relationship and current waits. Its implementation alone does not prove the requested late-bookkeeping outcome. | A completed attached child must be able to satisfy the wait belonging to that attachment after delayed bookkeeping or restart. Require one effective resume when that wait applies. A different winning trigger or terminal parent must stay authoritative. An earlier nonmatching rejection cannot count as a successful resume. Choice of coordination mechanism belongs to Architecture. |
| M1. Child reopens after a cascade | FR-8 and AD-07 allow correction to reopen progress-completed work. FR-10 applies a cascade to active descendants and skips those already terminal. Earlier decisions covered late attachment but left reopening after a completed cascade unresolved. | **Product decision, 20 November.** Preserve the correction and reopening events. If an attached ancestor was Cancelled or Expired, durably schedule the matching termination for the newly active descendant. Honor intervening valid acts, resume the outstanding cascade after restart and avoid duplicate effective termination on redelivery. Its own Remaining contributes while active and contributes zero after termination. |

The architecture’s registry and receipt protocols describe target behavior. The November audit retains the temporary single-writer restriction and requires competing-parent, failure and restart evidence before enabling multiple writers. ## Queries, authorization and performance

| Finding | What the sources establish | Consequence for the PRD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H3. Tenant-wide list | F2-H allowed an omitted executor ID to reveal all assignments. In the current SDK, an ordinary delegated request without the caller’s own verified PartyId returns Forbidden. A permitted non-delegated coordinator workload can request the tenant-wide list. | Align the candidate to FR-20 and §9. A verified delegated executor sees their own Assigned work and the tenant’s shared Queued work. Test a missing PartyId, a request using another PartyId and an explicitly permitted coordinator. Omitting the PartyId confers no coordinator access. |
| M4. Tenant casing | The Works dispatch adapter compares the envelope and command tenant case-insensitively and compares the item ID exactly. AD-15 specifies that the production trust boundary rejects noncanonical raw tenant input before deriving keys. | Exercise the same mixed-case envelope at both boundaries. Distinguish canonical-equivalence within the adapter from raw-input rejection at production ingress. Keep the strict rule a production-admission requirement. |
| M5. Single-item detail | The current SDK exposes same-tenant Queued or own Assigned detail to a verified delegated reader. Immediately after Claim the item becomes InProgress and its bound executor receives NotFound. A permitted coordinator can read the healthy same-tenant item. Missing authority returns Forbidden. Invalid projection evidence can return projection-unavailable. | **Product decision, 20 November.** For direct detail and its Roll-Up, the verified current bound tenant member may also read their own InProgress or Suspended item. A different ordinary member receives NotFound for those items. Keep existing Queued, own Assigned and authorized coordinator access. FR-20 list eligibility still covers shared Queued and own Assigned. The expanded detail rule is a target requiring implementation and authorization tests. |
| M3. Performance budgets | The PRD and NFR11 in the epics classify the five-second convergence and sub-200-ms read figures as benchmarks pending G8. AD-22 and forward candidates F4-C/F4-J can be read as enforcing them today. | Test correctness at acknowledged convergence. Record fixture timings. Product and Architecture own a future binding budget with defined workload, environment, percentile, measurement window and failure policy. |

The detail handler checks trusted authority before reading, then separately verifies membership, item visibility and projection consistency. A filtered result and an unavailable projection have different user-visible meanings. ## Effort, suspension, recovery and acceptance fixtures

| Finding | Source-backed resolution |
| --- | --- |
| H4. Report on an overrun | Story 5.1 must retain the raw positive delta. With 7 Done and a revised estimate of 4, a new accepted report leaves 7 Done and 0 Remaining. The progress event precedes progress-driven completion. Test an ordinary report with work remaining separately. |
| H5. Ordered suspension evidence | The approved September decision requires new writers to preserve the submitted order and exact duplicates, including both instances in a child/date/child example. Read legacy singular evidence as one member. The first matching Resume committed by the aggregate consumes the wait and clears all entries. Align F3-F and its compatibility criteria. |
| M2. Executor parity | SM-3 should compare domain event order, normalized payloads and resulting state across executor kinds. Verify the correct authenticated Party and trusted provenance separately for each fixture. Whole-envelope equality would erase expected audit differences. |
| L1. Restart and Remaining | Recover the last committed status, Unit and effort. Depending on prior events, an InProgress item may have positive Remaining, zero Remaining after re-estimation, or undefined Remaining until estimated. Restart itself changes none of these facts. |
| L2. Runnable lifecycle journey | Create with an estimate, assign or queue, claim as the authorized Party, then report partial progress before spawning the child. Confirm suspend, resume and completion against the durable event history. FR-1, FR-6 and FR-8 supply the prerequisites missing from FR-24 and SM-1. |

The September decision settled collection admission and concurrent matching. The November audit confirms the existing cumulative effort rule and supplies the representative replay sequence. Identity provenance belongs to the trusted envelope under FR-7 and §9.
