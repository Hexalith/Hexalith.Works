# Downstream consistency review — Hexalith.Works

**20 November 2026.** The revised architecture and forward acceptance criteria agree with the PRD on query access, effort replay, suspension and coordinated recovery. The active-item detail extension is an approved target that still needs implementation and conformance evidence.

**Open findings:** 0 critical, 0 high, 0 medium, 0 low.

## Query access and tenant identity

- **H3 · Coordinator list.** F2-H denies a delegated caller who omits PartyId or requests a colleague’s work. Supplying the caller’s verified PartyId returns their assigned items and the shared queue. Only an allow-listed, non-delegated coordinator workload authorized for the tenant gets the all-work list. A caller-supplied entitlement cannot widen access. [PRD FR-20 and §9](prd.md#9-cross-cutting-nfrs) · [Architecture AD-03](../../architecture.md) · [F2-H](../../epics.md#forward-candidate-f2-h-discover-the-next-work-deterministically)
- **M5 · Individual detail and Roll-Up.** The SDK checks caller authority before reading the projection. For an authorized ordinary member it currently returns detail for queued items and their own assigned items. An unauthorized item is indistinguishable from an absent one. The approved target extends detail access to the current Executor’s own in-progress and suspended work. Claim still removes the item from the list. Handoff transfers detail access to the incoming Executor. A stale binding cannot authorize the outgoing Executor. F4-F covers these outcomes and the SDK-to-target change. [PRD §9](prd.md#9-cross-cutting-nfrs) · [Architecture AD-03](../../architecture.md) · [F4-F](../../epics.md#forward-candidate-f4-f-authorize-commands-from-verified-identity) · [WorksQueryPolicy](../../../../src/Hexalith.Works/Queries/WorksQueryPolicy.cs) · [GetWorkItemQueryHandler](../../../../src/Hexalith.Works/Queries/GetWorkItemQueryHandler.cs)
- **M4 · Tenant casing.** The same lower-case payload tenant and upper-case envelope tenant can pass the isolated Works adapter’s comparison. Target ingress rejects the noncanonical envelope before dispatch. AD-15 and PRD §9 give the two test boundaries explicitly. [PRD §9](prd.md#9-cross-cutting-nfrs) · [Architecture AD-15](../../architecture.md) · [Works adapter](../../../../src/Hexalith.Works/WorkItemEventStoreAggregate.cs)

## Durable behavior and recovery

- **H4 · Existing effort overrun.** Story 5.1 distinguishes a report that consumes positive Remaining from one submitted after an estimate fell below Done. In the latter case the submitted one-hour delta is recorded, Done stays at seven against an estimate of four, and replay recovers the completion. [PRD FR-8](prd.md#fr-8-report-progress-and-complete) · [Story 5.1](../../epics.md#story-51-make-progress-and-event-ordinals-overflow-safe)
- **H5 and H6 · Suspension and child attachment.** AD-13, F3-F, F3-G and Story 5.2 preserve order and duplicates in the suspension event. The first matching Resume committed by the item wins. A fresh command carrying the last consumed condition can yield a domain no-op while the item is in progress. Transport redelivery with the original identity and content returns the recorded disposition. AD-21 and F3-E distinguish an ordinary Suspend from a fenced attachment that requests a child wait. The latter can suspend an in-progress parent or add the missing child to an existing suspension. A cleared flag preserves the active waits. Terminal parents acquire no wait. F3-G reconciles an early child completion across restart and prevents it from waking a later suspension. [PRD FR-14 through FR-16](prd.md#fr-14-suspend-on-an-ordered-collection-of-await-conditions) · [Architecture AD-13 and AD-21](../../architecture.md) · [Candidate criteria](../../epics.md) · [Story 5.2](../../epics.md#story-52-make-the-lifecycle-authority-executable-and-singular)
- **M1 · Reopening after a cascade.** AD-10 and F3-I retain the correction and reopen events of a descendant skipped when terminal. The Reactor durably issues the ancestor’s matching Cancel or Expire for this reopening and retries across restart. The active contribution returns to zero when termination commits. [PRD FR-10](prd.md#fr-10-cancel-reject-expire) · [Architecture AD-10](../../architecture.md) · [F3-I](../../epics.md#forward-candidate-f3-i-terminate-work-and-cascade-across-attached-descendants)
- **M3 · Performance evidence.** F4-C, F4-J and AD-22 require correct totals at acknowledged convergence. The fixture records convergence time and authorized read latency. G8 supplies the dataset, environment, percentile, window and failure policy before a timing becomes binding. [PRD §10](prd.md#101-v1-dependency-and-exit-gates) · [Architecture AD-22](../../architecture.md) · [F4-C and F4-J](../../epics.md)
