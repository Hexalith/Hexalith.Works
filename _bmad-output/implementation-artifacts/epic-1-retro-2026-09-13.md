### Process lessons

1. **A handler taking a nullable state needs a test per branch of that parameter.** Epic 1's
   `Handle(CreateWorkItem, state)` read `state` in one place; all 18 Range-A call sites passed `null`.
   Three review layers missed a defect that would have reset live Work Items.
2. **A story's check-off must be verifiable in that story's own commit.** Story 1.5 `[x]`'d remediations
   that arrived in Story 4.8's commit; Story 1.5 also logged Story 4.8's deferred work as its own. The
   same class of defect as Epic 4's Story 4.6 false-completion CRITICAL — this is now three occurrences.
3. **A fitness test that reads project-file text is a claim, not a guarantee.** Epic 1 shipped four such
   guards. The one that mattered most (G1) is green today over a live violation. Assert over the
   realized, restored build graph.
4. **Test-count drift starts at the first story.** Story 1.4's review corrected 52→60 and 24→25. Epic 3
   made it Action Item 1, Epic 4 scored it "partial". It began here.
