## Intent

**Problem:** EventStore has opt-in epoch/chunk code, but R3–R4 lack a published, store-proven delivery and rebuild contract that cannot lose acknowledged writes.

**Approach:** Complete per-envelope fan-out and the tenant/family epoch fence, prove recovery and generation selection, then release a named SDK version with a package-only API contract. Story 4.14 adopts it in Works.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** EventStore owns envelope validation, CAS epoch/journal/checkpoint, generation selection, retry/quarantine, and retention/offboarding; domain handlers own folds. Follow AD-16 fencing and AD-27 exclusive `FromSequence` paging with complete decoding. Use current durable types and synthetic data until AD-28 data-owner retention/legal-hold/offboarding approval and a restore drill authorize new durable types or non-synthetic data. Record the package version and contract evidence.

**Never:** Depend on Works payloads, skip undecodable/conflicting evidence, acknowledge an unfenced write, expose staging, or remove Works hosting before Stories 4.14 and 4.16.

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Expected Output / Behavior | Error Handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concurrent rebuild | Registered writers deliver during capture, Building, and Commit | One committed generation remains selected; journaled positions catch up before fresh reads | Stale lease or epoch fails before acknowledgement |
| Duplicate or conflict | Same stream/position redelivered | Identical digest is idempotent | Different digest is durably quarantined and readiness degrades |
| Interrupted chunk | Reservation exists but chunk or checkpoint is incomplete after restart | Source replay completes or safely disposes the position; no lost acknowledged write | Bounded retry, durable unresolved state, no silent cleanup |
| Large history | At least 10,000 captured streams, staged mutations, and delivered positions | Bounded chunks/batches recover with exact member counts | Oversize or corruption fails closed without partial promotion |
